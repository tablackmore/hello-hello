A book by Nigel Blackmore

Hello, hello, hello Good God, any God

This book is an exploration of spiritual and religious beliefs throughout mankind’s history. It investigates the reasons for such beliefs and considers whether there is evidence for a God, of any sort. In the process, besides religion, it examines other knowledge sources - philosophy and science.
