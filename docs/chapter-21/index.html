Chapter 21

Design a God

Pages 220–230

If there is a Supreme Deity, a God of the sort described in Abrahamic religions, many would say He does not operate in quite the way many would wish. HE has been much too laissez-faire for most peoples liking. What’s he done about all the bad things that have happened in the past and continue to occur? Nothing identifiable is the answer. Think of human history, the many, many wars, their associated destruction and wasted lives. Then there’s the disasters; disease; poverty; famine; repression; fear; brutal treatment; inequality and restricted freedom? A number of these harmful occurrences are the result of mistreatment by some humans of other humans, although the vagaries of climate, plate tectonics, cosmology, micro-organisms and accidents play their part too. And while we are listing all the bads of life, we should remember that humans have not only quite often made life miserable for their fellow kind, they’ve also had a detrimental effect on the planet, through relentless exploitation of all its resources including other inhabitants. We’ve also managed to speed up Climate Change. Rapid global warming is causing more problems for humankind.

Just two of the diseases that have left a big, adverse, mark on humanity are the Black Death of the 14th century and much more recently the Coronavirus, COVID 19.

In the 14th century the Black Death killed 50 million people, 60% of Europe’s population, and possibly 200 million worldwide. COVID 19 virus caused havoc the world over in 2020 but actual deaths were nowhere near that of its Black Death predecessor 600 years earlier.

p.221

Up to January 2021 COVID 19 deaths worldwide were about 2.5 million. That is only 1.25% of the Black Death total.

To sum up, there’s been a never-ending litany of events, and personal circumstances, which have resulted in so much despair; so many painful, early deaths and too many miserable, tragic and sometimes very short human existences.

Though, to be fair, it’s by no means all been bad. Lots of people have led interesting, happy, long and fulfilling lives. Even these, and those people who might be considered the fortunate ones: the relatively rich; the healthy, the successful and contented have not got away scot-free. No one goes through life without problems at some stage. At the very least, the process of dying, or of some loved one dying, is generally considered to be an unwanted experience. But we all die.

Where has the traditional God been when all this nastiness has been going on? If he did set up the universe and if He created and loves humans as most mainstream religions claim why hasn’t he made life much easier for everyone?

Theologians and devout religious people often say that “We cannot know God’s will”, in answer to His failure to prevent dreadful occurrences. This assumes that no matter what happens it will all be for the best in the long term. Such an approach is not helpful in many circumstances. Tell the “God’s will” argument to a mother who has to watch helpless as her much loved child dies slowly of some fatal disease; perhaps in pain and debilitated for years before the final moment comes. Only the blinkered, extreme religious devout, can thank God in these circumstances.

We can confidently say our traditional God is most notable by His absence from the lives of men and women when they are most in need of Him.

Another thing, He is also far too serious. He lacks a sense of humour and lightness of touch. HE never sees the funny side. Laughter completely eludes him; despite he designed us, purportedly, and should be aware of its importance to humans.

Of course, all this presupposes that there is in fact some sort of God. It may be, though that He exists purely as a thought in a human brain - a stream of elementary particles, faintly flickering among the neural pathways of religious people. That certainly explains why He is so insubstantial and uninvolved. - just a thought!

So, if any current God is not much cop, why don’t we have a new one? Is this possible? - well yes, it is, sort of, maybe.

What do we want from our new God? If we are to have a different deity, ideally, we want one who is considerably more active and sympathetic than the traditional monotheistic one, HE needs to be a super-being who will ensure nasty things do not happen; to anyone, ever.

Designing Our New God

p.222

Any God that we can personally design will be quite different from previous models. Our God’s universe will be wholly fun rather than holy glum. God’s default state will be constantly friendly and sympathetic towards humanity - no killing earthquakes, tidal waves, hurricanes, droughts or other harmful climatic events. No wars; diseases; injuries or famines either.

HE will also arrange things in such a way so that a line is drawn over which humans cannot step to do hurt to others, themselves. or indeed to harm our beautiful planet. “Cruelty” and “suffering” will become redundant words and expunged from every dictionary. Specific parameters will be set down, in the form of a contract describing what humans want of God and, conversely, what God requires of humans.

First, let’s look at what the new God needs of humans in the contract. Just like Isaac Asimov’s “three laws of robotics” there will be “three laws of God”, which humans will feel compelled to adhere to. Well, of course there will always be a lot more than three laws, otherwise governments and parliaments would suffer extreme deprivation symptoms and lawyers would be fewer and poorer. But our three laws will be fundamental, basic core laws representing the divine will. They will apply to all humans at all times and all where. These are the statutes of our customised God, HE Requires All humans to:

  1. Be fair to and help their fellow man or woman. If you treat others fairly and helpfully they are more likely to reciprocate and everyone will be happier;

  2. Take an interest in a wide range of things. It’s an amazing world out there, find out as much about it and the wider universe as you can. The more you know, the better the life decisions you will make. Wider knowledge is also the antipathy of rigid, dogmatic thinking and actions;

  3. Look after the world they have inherited. It’s a moral imperative that we should hand it on to future generations in good, enjoyable and utilisable shape.

p.223

Humans will be slightly re-designed so that their natural state is a tendency to adhere to these laws.

The new God will head up a new religion, with all the best traditional trimmings to keep humans intrigued and amused. There will be interesting rituals and rites to pursue. These will be designed to be more enjoyable and entertaining than those of any current religion. Humans find it essential to their religious belief to engage in some form of ritual, often associated with eating or not eating some sort of food’, like eating fish on a Friday, or not eating pork - ever. Then there must be some designated special days, perhaps with dressing up parades or giving/receiving presents and general jolly making. To allow humans to feel comfortable with their newly designed God a specific food ritual will be made desirable, but not compulsory. It will be a requirement to eat peanut butter on toast for breakfast every Monday. This will be no problem for those who like peanut butter. For those that don’t, it will be just one more thing to endure on an already disliked day of the week, Monday. And once Monday is over, all can be rosier for the rest of the week. In fact, our new God will ensure that this is so.

We will also establish a specific Godly emblem. This will be a circlet of carrots and butterflies on a star shaped greenery background. The emblem will emphasise our new God’s environmental stance and healthy eating credentials. We will have feasting and drinking at celebratory days mid-winter and mid-summer. This will allow people plenty of time to recover in between and give them something to look forward to. These summer and winner festivities will provide the retail trade and entertainments industry a boost.

On God’s side of the contract humans will be made bullet proof against serious diseases, accidents and all other unpleasant events. The average length of a very happy life will be 150 years. Death will become a truly comfortable, transitional, going-away-on-an-endless-holiday type experience. All defunct bodies will be composted and used to grow carrots and greens. These vegetables will help humans see the light and be in keeping with the Godly emblem. However, on the right side of the line humans will still retain what they think of as free will.

Why can’t we have such a Supreme Being who looks after us like this? Well, good news - we can! Like many, many humans before us we can take this basic template and draw up detailed designs for our own God and his buttressing religion. For tens of thousands of years, in fact throughout the whole of human history, man has invented a personal God or more usually, several Gods or Spirits that they feel comfortable with. We’ve had, and still have, hundreds of different religions. Even within the same religion people have held their own personal idea of who and what their God is. The effect of multiplying each main stream religion by the number of derivations or sects and then again by every adherent’s individual take means we have had a myriad of personalised deities. We can continue this well-established trend - set the detailed criteria for a God we want, building on the broad contractual basis listed above. All our ancestors and contemporaries have done this sort of thing. But this time, we can work hard to get it right, with the help of a selected cadre of geniuses, all of them carefully programmed.

The God Commissioning Committee

p.224

This bunch of super-people will form a committee - a God Commissioning Committee.

The God Commissioning Committee’s aim will be to work up the basic contract into a fully-fledged set of detailed requirements. The possible committee members will be carefully screened to ensure no rigid, narrow-minded extremists make the cut.

The committee will be required to ensure our God is imbued with a full set of attractive attributes that allow Him/Her (androgynous of course) to be a truly thoughtful; kind; benevolent; just; forgiving; comforting and - new feature - amusing deity. He will be a joke cracking, laughing God, that’s essential. If He is judged to be too serious or solemn He will be subject to a fine, or up to six months community service. Fun loving will be His compulsory default state. In short, our God will be someone you can occasionally sit down with and enjoy a pint in a pub, a glass of wine in a trendy bistro or even a Cappuccino in Costabucks.

In effect, He will be a God with sufficient capability, attributes and resolve to keep everyone happy; on Earth as well as in heaven.

Incidentally, the type of heaven that individuals desire will be based on their personal choice. All recently departed persons (RDPs for short) will initially float, as a sentient spiritual essence, to a transit station. Here, they will be asked to browse a set of 3D holographic travelogues.

p.225

These will espouse the attractions of a variety of heavens. To get the RDP in the right mood Cliff Richard’s song “We Are All Going on a Summer Holiday” will play in the background. The angel Ezekiel will offer advice and guidance to every RDP. He will use expertise gained from a months practical training course with a major travel company.

The RDP would choose from the 99 heavens available in the travelogues. These will include all the well-known ones like: the Vikings Valhalla; Buddhists Nirvana: Confucians Tian; Muslims Jennah or Paradise (although virgins will be banned, but Virgin TV and virgin olive oil allowed).

Then there’s the Swarga Loka of Hindus and the traditional Christian heaven. The latter would be at the back, in the special offers section. As well as all these there would be lots of others to choose from. After confirming selection, RDPs will be immediately transferred to his/her heaven of choice. Later, to avoid endless tedium, all RDPs will be allowed to have temporary or permanent respite by spending time in alternate heavens to their original choice.

Some RDPs, who had not cut the mustard when pre-RDP, (i.e., despite Godly persuasion, they had been naughty people on Earth) will be automatically moved out of the transit station into the rehab centre. There will be no hell, just a rehabilitation centre that works.

After a suitable re-conditioning period the contrite and spiritually restored RDP will be shunted back from re-hab to transit and given license to choose his/her heaven.

Fred Satan will have a much curtailed and very minor role in human affairs. He will be reduced in rank, and become just an annoying little devil, one who can easily be slapped down.

Let’s start the in-depth Godly design process in good old British administrative fashion, with lining up the God Commissioning Committee.

The committee’s remit will be to consider the aim and broad requirement already indicated and develop it in more detail. They will be given one year to complete this task. Over the course of this year, with monthly meetings, and input from various sub-groups, our committee will draw up a detailed specification for a God, including his modus operandi. They will also produce an easy-to-read manual, a timetable of religious festivals or special observance days, a list of foods that should or should not be eaten, a dress code and a marketing plan. These would establish him as a serious Deity (not too serious of course. There must always be laughter). The marketing package will get Him and His message across to the whole world.

Once the whole process is put in train, with advertising on prime-time TV, and all media, an ever-increasing number of believers in our God will be recruited. This rising tide of belief will eventually reach Tsunami proportions. The mystic power generated by this mass belief will become so great our God will emerge as a reality - of sorts. Instead of the excessively humble religious maxim “thy will be done” it will be a case of “thy done be willed” (in old English vernacular).

p.226

Key features of the proposed God specification will be drawn up in a White, Black, Yellow and Brown Paper. This discriminatory free, consultative document will be subject to a series of votes by every Earth person over the age of 10 (children have a say in this too).

Here are the God Commissioning Committee members:

Chairman: Archangel Gabriel, Celestial. Archie Gabriel has close-up, first-hand knowledge of a God, warts and all. He is also the guardian of “truth”, which can be something of a double-edged sword. Hence, he wields one of these. Gabriel, with his personalised transportation plumage, is never held up by traffic problems. He will therefore set a good example by always being on time for committee meetings. Of course, his limited golfing prowess is a bit of handicap for a senior committee man, with so many decisions commonly taken on the golf course, or in the club bar. But being a wimp on the links should not detract too much. He’s certain to have the right balls for this job;

Secretary: William Shakespeare, English. Bill is someone who has the most extreme ability to convey the written word in a timeless fashion to the whole world. He will draw up reports giving the committee’s reasoning and conclusions for all Earth to see. Most importantly, he will draft a moral guide book on behalf of our new God. This will commonly be found unread and in pristine condition in every hotel bedroom on Earth. It will be called “The Babble”;

Assistant Secretary: Charles Dickens, English. Like Bill, Charlie is also pretty handy with a pen. His particular skill is to convey the triumph of goodness and philanthropy in mesmeric prose - remember Oliver Twist and a Christmas Carol among others. Charlie will help temporise the danger of any dark and difficult Macbethian; Hamletian or Othelloian writing style that Bill may be tempted to employ;

p.227

Treasurer: David Hume, Scottish. Dave brings a lot to the party. He is a philosopher, historian and an economist. Once the new God is in place, there is no one better than Dave to say exactly what God is, although he won’t be so hot in speculating what God ought to be. In his time (the 18th century) he expressed doubts about the common proofs for God’s, i.e. the Christian one’s, existence. He also pooh-poohed the link between morality and religion and he scoffed at miracles. These attributes will be useful. His renowned scepticism is ameliorated by a strong streak of realism. God will clearly have to be plugged into a viable, realistic, historical context. Empirical Dave will certainly cause this to happen. In addition, Dave’s pragmatic approach will help, together with Socs, (see below) ensure the committee don’t get too carried away into esoteric flights of fancy. He will also have the specific job of keeping an eye on the committee’s budget, as Treasurer. Scots are meant to be good with money. Hopefully the almost totally credit crunched Scottish banks RBS and HBOS of 2009 are not typical of Scottish financial acumen;

Head of Marketing: St. Paul (reverting to Saul) Paul’s letters to several different and mysterious “ians” i.e. Corinthians, Galatians, Philippians etc. - curiously, none of the ians came from Scotland - have been read out in churches all over the world for thousands of years. He certainly knows how to get a message across to the masses. Never mind that his past thoughts and writings on topics such as an early version of “God the divine watchmaker”; humanity’s “original sin”; and “the end of the world is nigh” have put forward distinctly dodgy propositions or predictions, that is to say: they were Wholly, with a capital W, in error. And his views on women will make for interesting committee banter with Mary W., a fearsome feminist. No matter, all that’s needed to set Paul on a new, enlightened path is a another very bright light in the sky followed by a little bit of re-education. It will then be a new dawn for Paul. To signify his re-branding Paul will revert to his original name - Saul. The reinvented Saul will be able to mix it fabulously with the best of the theological and philosophical luvvies;

Other Committee members:

Socrates, Greek. Socs will talk to anyone about anything. He asks endless, forensically probing questions and through this technique strips away the veneer of hypocrisy, prejudice and misunderstanding so that people arrive closer to their own truth;

Epicurus, This ancient Greek philosopher will insist committee meetings are held in the garden. The natural beauty of the plants and outdoor scenery will help foster a calm, relaxed attitude. Committee members, will be encouraged to make the most of every day and ensure the new God supports the natural environment, the pursuit of happiness and explains why not to fear death;

p.228

Isaac Newton and Albert Einstein, English and German. Zac and Bert were two of the greatest scientific minds of all time. They will ensure the new God has a firm scientific, provable base. Too long has God sat, in most religions, at the frontiers of science. Quite often the religious devout have found it necessary to ignore, dismiss or bury uncomfortable scientific facts. Sometimes, they’ve buried both the facts and the bearer of them. We won’t have any of that nonsense with our new God. Zac and Bert will help design a God who is within, not outside, scientific boundaries. He will be a God who can be fitted comfortably within a new, Great Theory of Everything (also known as the Big Toe theory). Zac will bring to these considerations his gravitas and Bert his relatives;

John Locke, English John was a liberal philosopher. His core beliefs were in the rights and freedom of the individual, but he also recognised the general responsibilities of the individual to society as whole. With these ideas he has the perfect qualifications for a committee member. His concept of a social contract between individuals and those in authority (the state) underpinned both the French and American revolutions. This can similarly help in formulating the contract between God and humans. John will probably want to borrow the Tardis time machine to consult somewhat like-minded people such as Thomas Hobbes and Jean-Jacques Rousseau, although he might find the former too fierce in his views of human nature and the latter too soft;

René Descartes, French. René was both a ground-breaking philosopher and great mathematician. In the past he and Zac have seen the light differently. He was also a big fan of the tortuous ontological argument for God’s existence. Despite, or perhaps because of this, he can help Zac and Bert in their empirical approach. René’s rationalism, of sorts, will also be a useful counterfoil to Dave. He can check Dave’s sums in a role of assistant Treasurer;

Astrid Lindgren, Swedish. This author knows how to reach out to children. Her Pippi Longstocking books have been translated into 85 languages, published in more than 100 countries and sold 145 million copies worldwide. With her influence, the new God will be child friendly and definitely not child frightening. Astrid will get His message across to the young in a way they will like and understand;

p.229

Mary Wollstonecraft, English, Mary was a philosopher and early feminist with a strong unconventional streak. There will be no discriminatory element in the new God’s make up with Mary on the design team. There will be none of this: “The Jews are the chosen people”, lark or: “It’s all Eve’s fault that Adam ate that apple.”, which is even larkier. She will swiftly disabuse Saul of his implied view that women are quite fortunate to reach even 2nd class status;

Terry Pratchett and Giovanni Guareschi, English and Italian. These two hugely talented novelists will bring humour and a gentle, humane, quirkiness to God’s character. Their respective books, in the Discworld and Don Camillo series, amply demonstrate these qualities. They will be delegated the role of writing the chapter in Bill’s report that cover these aspects of God’s persona;

Averroes (Latin name) or Ibn Rushd (Arabic), Cordoban, Spaniard or Moroccan - take your pick. Ibn was an Islamic theologian; lawyer; philosopher; astronomer; mathematician; physician; musical theorist and other things too. With such a brilliant, wide ranging intellect Ibn is the quintessential polymath. Incidentally, a polymath is not a parrot who is good at sums. Most importantly, Averroes is seen as the unintentional founder of Western secularism. This is the principal reason that he gets the nod here over his immensely talented predecessor, Avicenna. Averroes has the perfect background to ensure the new God is acceptable to both East and West. He will arrange so that our God can be found via religious, philosophical, scientific and secular routes. There will be no slaughtering by believers on one road of other believers who are following a different route, while aiming at the same destination. “All roads lead to God.” will become a truism with Ibn’s help;

Leonardo da Vinci, Italian: a multi-faceted genius of the first order. His paintings and inventions are unique and priceless. Lennie will be the supreme illustrator for the Babble, which Bill will write. In the margins of this book Lennie will be given license to draw doodles of complicated machinery, which will take hundreds of years to become properly invented. He will also work with Astrid to write a children’s version of the Babble. It will be comic book in style, with both Bambi and super-heroes fitted in; difficult, but not impossible for someone of Leonardo’s ability. No doubt this will appeal to tiny tots, through to teenagers and even those many adults who are children at heart or who can’t read. Everyone can use it;

This is a formidable line up of talent. They will all be brought forward to modern times, with the help of Dr Who’s Tardis, a time travelling machine. Each will be plucked from their era when at the height of their mental powers. They will initially spend one year on a foundation course. This will bring them up to speed on all the latest scientific and philosophical thinking. Cosmology; evolution; particle physics; genetics and computer studies would be covered in depth. The training will include all aspects of the modern world, especially the Internet, social media, and governing structures. After one year, they will get down to serious work.

p.230

The committee will need a clerk to do the mundane administrative stuff, and make the tea. The writer wishes to volunteer for this role. Here’s our God Commissioning committee in summary:

Chairman: Archangel Gabriel; Secretary: William Shakespeare; Asst. Secretary: Charles Dickens; Treasurer: David Hume; Asst. Treasurer: René Descartes; Head of Marketing: Saul (St Paul)

Other Committee Members: [Note: normally these would be called “Ordinary Committee Members”, but there is nothing ordinary about these people. They are all extraordinary in the extreme] Socrates; Epicurus; Isaac Newton; Albert Einstein; John Locke; Astrid Lindgren; Mary Wollstonecraft; Terry Pratchett; Giovanni Guareschi; Averroes; Leonardo da Vinci. What a God they would create.
